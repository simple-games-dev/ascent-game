# Ascent

A strategy game for **2 or 3 players** where you move your pawns around the board and build your way up.

## 🎮 Play it [here](https://simple-games-dev.github.io/ascent-game/)

## 🏗️ Rules

- The game can be played with **2 or 3 players**.
- Each player has **2 pawns**, placed at the start of the game.
- On your turn, choose one of your pawns and **move it to an empty neighbouring tile**. Diagonal movement is allowed.
- You can:
  - Move **down any number of storeys**
  - Stay on the **same level**
  - Move **up by at most 1 storey**
- You **cannot move onto an occupied tile** or onto a capped **4-storey roof**.
- After moving, you must **build 1 storey** on an empty tile next to your pawn's new position.
- Buildings can be built up to **4 storeys**.
- If one of your pawns reaches the **3rd storey**, you **win immediately**.
- If none of your pawns can make a legal move on your turn, you're **out of the game**.

Move, build, block your opponents, and climb to the top.
